GE tax used here: 2% of the sale price per item, rounded down (so items sold for under 50 gp pay nothing), capped at 5,000,000 gp per item. Exempt items (—, e.g. Old school bond, Energy potions, low-level food, teleport tablets, basic tools) pay no tax. Source: OSRS Wiki, Grand Exchange: convenience fee and Items exempt from Grand Exchange tax, checked — (UTC date).

Data: prices, volumes and buy limits come from the OSRS Wiki real-time prices API (/latest, /5m, /24h, /timeseries, /mapping), fetched directly by your browser and cached as the API's headers allow. Routes are parsed from wiki pages by tools/build_routes.py, Crafting Profits recipes by tools/build_recipes.py; news mentions come from a dated snapshot of the official feed (tools/fetch_news.py).

No trackers, no analytics, no cookies. Your filters and watchlist stay in this browser (localStorage). Not affiliated with Jagex or the OSRS Wiki. Always check prices in-game before trading.
