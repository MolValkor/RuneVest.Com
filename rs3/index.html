Flip calculator Not financial advice

Enter the prices your offers actually fill at in-game. The guide price is only a starting point. Tax: 2% of the sale price per item, rounded down, paid by the seller; sales under 50 coins and bonds pay none (RuneScape Wiki, Trade tariff). The wiki documents no per-item cap for RS3 (OSRS caps tax at 5m).
